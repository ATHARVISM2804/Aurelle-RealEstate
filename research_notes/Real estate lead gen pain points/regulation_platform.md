# Regulatory & Platform-Policy Constraints: Real Estate Website + 3D Floor Plans + AI WhatsApp/Web Chatbot + Meta Lead Ads + Lead-Scoring CRM (India & UAE, 2025-2026)

Research date: 1 November 2026. Scope: India and UAE only. Official sources were preferred where reachable. Several official PDFs and portals (TRAI, MahaRERA, UP-RERA) blocked automated fetches. In those cases the notes rely on law-firm or trade-press summaries, and say so.

## 1. India RERA advertising rules (registration number, QR codes, state rules, penalties, claims, carpet area)

### Takeaway
Section 11(2) of the RERA Act requires every advertisement to show the project's RERA registration number and the authority's website address. Several states now add a mandatory scannable QR code in a prescribed position, usually the top-right. These include Maharashtra (since 1 Aug 2023, tightened 8 Apr 2025), Gujarat (15 Jun 2025), Tamil Nadu (1 Jul 2025), Kerala and Goa. The rules expressly cover social media and WhatsApp. Vague disclaimers ("T&C apply"), "X+ amenities" counts and travel-time claims are now banned in TN and Goa. Every template the product generates (web page, Meta ad creative, WhatsApp brochure, chatbot message carrying a brochure) needs a per-state "RERA block".

### Cited Findings
- **Central law (Section 11(2)).** Promoters must show the project registration number and the RERA website in advertisements. GujRERA and MahaRERA guidance both treat this as already compulsory under the Act; the QR code is a state-level addition — [Housivity on GujRERA QR mandate](https://housivity.com/blog/how-gujreras-qr-code-mandate-is-changing-real-estate-marketing-in-gujarat); [Free Press Journal on MahaRERA](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharera-mandates-prominent-display-of-regulatory-details-in-real-estate-ads)
- **Section 12 (false advertisement).** An allottee misled by an advertisement or prospectus can withdraw and get their full investment back with interest, plus compensation — [Housing.com on Sec 12](https://housing.com/news/what-is-the-promoters-responsibility-as-per-section-12-of-the-rera-act). In one TNRERA case, a false title claim in a brochure was held to breach Sec 12 — [SS Rana](https://ssrana.in/corporate-laws/rera-real-estate-laws-india/penalties-rera-india/)
- **Section 61 penalty.** Breach of any provision other than Sections 3 and 4 can draw a penalty of up to 5% of the estimated project cost. Secondary sources disagree on whether advertising breaches are charged under Sec 61/63 or Sec 60 — [SS Rana](https://ssrana.in/corporate-laws/rera-real-estate-laws-india/penalties-rera-india/); [ATB Legal](https://atblegal.com/blog/india-rera/penalties-and-liability/)
- **MahaRERA (Maharashtra), QR origin.** Order 46/2023 (29 May 2023) introduced a QR code for every registered project, mandatory in promotions from 1 Aug 2023. Order 46A/2023 (25 Jul 2023) set a penalty of Rs 10,000 to Rs 50,000; a breach not cured within 10 days is treated as continuing. Order 46B/2023 (21 Aug 2023) requires the QR to be legible and detectable by software and placed beside the registration number — [TaxGuru summary of MahaRERA orders](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/matter-non-display-quick-response-qr-code-promotions-advertisement-real-estate-projects-registered-maharera.html); [The Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/regulatory/maharera-makes-qr-code-registration-number-mandatory-in-ads-50000-penalty-for-violations/)
- **MahaRERA circular of 8 Apr 2025** (effective immediately):
  - The registration number, QR code and MahaRERA website must sit in the **top-right quadrant**, in a contrasting colour.
  - The font must be at least as large as the largest font used for the project's contact details.
  - The circular applies to "newspapers, leaflets, booklets, electronic media, banners, hoardings, social media, WhatsApp and similar platforms".
  - Penalty of up to Rs 50,000.
  - Source: [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharera-mandates-prominent-display-of-regulatory-details-in-real-estate-ads); [Rentu / Mumbai Property Exchange, 14 Apr 2025](https://rentu.in/property-news/maharashtra-rera-mandates-clear-display-of-project-registration-details-in-housin/1497)
- **MahaRERA enforcement:**
  - July 2024: action against 628 projects, fines of Rs 88.9 lakh (Rs 72.35 lakh collected) — [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/maharera-fines-628-projects-for-not-displaying-qr-code-registration-number/)
  - January 2026: an Akola developer was fined Rs 10,000 for a blurred QR code and registration number, and a missing website address, in a newspaper ad — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/maharera-fines-promoter-for-publishing-newspaper-ad-without-clear-rera-registration-and-qr-code-518299)
- **MahaRERA x ASCI.** The regulator has collaborated with ASCI to identify violations in real estate ads — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/real-estate-regulatory-authority-collaborates-with-asci-to-identify-violations-in-ads-23498.htm)
- **Gujarat (GujRERA Order 108).** QR code mandatory in ads from 15 Jun 2025 — [Housivity](https://housivity.com/blog/how-gujreras-qr-code-mandate-is-changing-real-estate-marketing-in-gujarat); enforcement reported by [Geosquare](https://www.geosquare.in/gujarat-rera-cracks-down-on-developers-for-qr-code-violations-897901152671-news)
- **Tamil Nadu (TNRERA order of 1 Jul 2025, amending Reg. 6 of the TNRERA General Regulations 2018):**
  - All media, including digital and social, must carry the registration number, a QR code and the TNRERA website, in the **top-right corner** at **12pt minimum**.
  - The ad must also show the builder's name, office address, contact details and the accurate project location.
  - Vague disclaimers such as "terms and conditions apply" are banned, as are vague claims like "100+ amenities" and travel-time claims. Only the actual distance by the common route may be stated.
  - Projects exempt from RERA must say so and give the reason.
  - Penalties under Sec 61/63.
  - Sources: [Mondaq, new TN guidelines](https://www.mondaq.com/india/real-estate/1649286/new-guidelines-for-real-estate-advertisements-in-tamil-nadu-ensuring-transparency-and-compliance); [The Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/regulatory/tnrera-tightens-real-estate-ad-rules-travel-time-vague-disclaimers-banned/); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/tnrera-cracks-down-on-misleading-real-estate-ads-with-tough-new-guidelines-73314.htm)
- **Goa RERA (reported 19 Aug 2025):**
  - No disclaimer clauses such as "terms and conditions apply" in print or online ads.
  - Amenities must match the agreement or brochure; a bare count such as "11+ amenities" is "prima facie misleading".
  - Only the approved-plan location may be used, with no travel times.
  - The registration number goes at the top-right of all promotional material.
  - The regulator signalled it would curb "representational" luxury-facility images.
  - Source: [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/goa-real-estate-authority-bars-disclaimers-in-property-ads-tightens-rules-on-amenities-79092.htm)
- **Kerala RERA.** QR code mandatory from 1 Sep (year per Housing.com headline), placed next to the registration number and website — [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/qr-code-mandatory-for-real-estate-projects-from-sep-1-kerala-rera/)
- **Uttar Pradesh (UP-RERA guidelines).** The registration number and portal name must appear "in big and bold fonts in a prominent place". Burying the RERA number in the disclaimer is listed as a violation — [Housing.com, UP-RERA ad guidelines](https://housing.com/news/up-rera-issues-project-advertisement-guidelines/) (page returned HTTP 406; details from search snippet only)
- **Delhi RERA.** Reported to be planning a mandate for carpet-area-only disclosure in place of super built-up area in ads — [The Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/regulatory/delhi-rera-to-mandate-carpet-area-disclosure-in-place-of-super-built-up-area-in-property-ads/)
- **Carpet area.** Under RERA, sale price must be tied to carpet area (net usable floor area, excluding common areas and balconies). Developers may still mention super built-up area but must also disclose carpet area — [Address Advisors explainer](https://addressadvisors.com/blog/what-is-rera-carpet-area); [TaxGuru on misleading ads](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/esay-mean-misleading-real-estate-ads-rera-asci.html)
- **Renders and brochure mismatch.** Buyers rely on renderings of gardens, clubhouses and carpet-area figures. Mismatches between brochure and site drive RERA complaints, and one-sided disclaimers are generally unenforceable where bargaining power is unequal — [Khurana & Khurana](https://www.khuranaandkhurana.com/when-the-brochure-lies-rera-complaints-over-brochure-to-site-mismatch)

### Inferences
- **Product requirement: a "RERA compliance block" component.** It needs the registration number, a QR image linking to the state RERA project page, and the authority URL. It should support state-specific placement rules: top-right for MH, TN and Goa, a minimum 12pt font for TN, and a font no smaller than the contact-details font for MH. Render it automatically on every landing page, ad creative export, PDF brochure and WhatsApp media message.
- **Do not let the chatbot or ad copy use banned phrases.** Block "T&C apply", "X+ amenities", "15 minutes from airport" and similar via lint rules, at least for TN and Goa projects. Prefer specific amenity lists sourced from the registered brochure or agreement.
- **3D floor plans and AI renders** should show RERA carpet area prominently (and balcony area separately). They should match the approved plan, because Sec 12 creates refund and compensation exposure for any representation that differs from what is delivered.
- **Possession dates and prices** quoted by the chatbot should be pulled only from the RERA registration (declared completion date) and the developer's approved price sheet. Unsupported promises can become Sec 12 "advertisement or prospectus" representations.
- **Gate projects without RERA registration.** RERA Section 3 bars advertising unregistered projects; this is general statutory knowledge and was not separately verified in this session. Gate ad creation on a registration number being present, or on an explicit "exempt project + reason" flag (required in TN).

### Gaps
- No primary text was retrieved for Karnataka (K-RERA) or Haryana (HRERA) ad orders. No reliable sources confirmed whether these states mandate QR codes.
- The UP-RERA guideline date and penalty could not be verified (fetch blocked).
- The Kerala QR-mandate year is not confirmed from the fetched text.
- **ASCI.** The text of an ASCI real-estate-specific guideline was not found. Only general ASCI disclaimer guidelines and the MahaRERA–ASCI collaboration were confirmed. Whether ASCI has a specific "artist's impression" or AI-render rule is unknown.
- **Section numbers.** Exact section numbers for advertising penalties are inconsistent across secondary sources (Sec 60 vs 61 vs 63). Verify against the Act text on indiacode.nic.in.

## 2. India DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

### Takeaway
The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 (Gazette: 14 Nov 2025) and roll out in phases:
- **Immediately:** the Board and definitions.
- **13 Nov 2026:** consent-manager registration (Rule 4).
- **13 May 2027:** core consent, notice, security, breach, retention and rights obligations. AZB gives 12 May 2027.

Until then, the IT Act s.43A / SPDI Rules 2011 continue to apply. For a lead-gen CRM, the developer or brokerage is the Data Fiduciary and the SaaS vendor is a Data Processor. Products should build now for a standalone, purpose-mapped consent notice, an easy withdrawal link, 72-hour breach reporting, 1-year log retention and 90-day grievance resolution.

### Cited Findings
- **Notification and phasing.** Notified 13 Nov 2025.
  - Immediately: definitions and Board provisions.
  - After 1 year (13 Nov 2026): consent-manager registration and operation.
  - After 18 months (13 May 2027): consent and related provisions, Data Fiduciary and SDF obligations, and the Board's remaining powers.
  - The IT Act s.43A and SPDI Rules 2011 stay in force until the 18-month mark. Sec 72-A of the IT Act (wrongful disclosure in breach of consent) is unaffected.
  - Source: [Khaitan & Co ERGO, 15 Nov 2025](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
  - On dates: Gazette publication was 14 Nov 2025 per [JSA Prism](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/JSA-Prism-InfoTech-November-2025-DPDP-Rules.Final_.pdf). The 18-month end date is given as 13 May 2027 in most sources and 12 May 2027 by AZB, per [search summary of Conventus/AZB](https://conventuslaw.com/featured-content/building-indias-digital-privacy-framework-an-overview-of-the-dpdp-rules-2025/) — minor conflict.
- **Notice (Rule 3).** The notice must be understandable on its own, separate from T&Cs or contracts. At minimum it must contain:
  - an itemised description of the personal data;
  - the specified purpose(s);
  - a "specific" description of the goods or services enabled. The final rules changed the draft's "itemized" to "specific".
  - It must also give a link to withdraw consent, as easy as giving it, and to exercise rights.
  - Source: [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Consent standard.** Consent must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and given by clear affirmative action — [search summary of DPDP Rules commentary](https://www.mondaq.com/india/privacy-protection/1707558/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-highlights)
- **Security safeguards.** The measures listed "such as" encryption, obfuscation, masking and tokenisation are indicative, alongside access controls, monitoring and logging, continuity, and retention of logs for at least 1 year for breach detection — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Mandatory minimum retention (new Rule 8(3)).** Personal data, traffic data and logs from processing must be kept for at least 1 year, even after the purpose is fulfilled or the account is deleted, for Seventh Schedule purposes (state or legal functions) — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Maximum 3-year retention (Third Schedule).** Applies only to e-commerce entities and social media intermediaries with 20M+ Indian users, and online gaming intermediaries with 5M+. They must give 48 hours' notice before erasure — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Breach notification.** An initial notice to the Board without undue delay, then a detailed report within 72 hours. Affected individuals must be told without delay; no fixed deadline is set for them — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Grievances** must be resolved within 90 days at most. Individuals may nominate someone to exercise rights on their behalf — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Children (under 18).** Verifiable parental consent is required, for example via DigiLocker tokens or age details already held — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Cross-border.** The government may notify restrictions on transfers to foreign states or entities they control. Significant Data Fiduciaries may face localisation of personal and traffic data on a committee's recommendation. No blacklist has been notified yet — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf)
- **Consent managers.** Must be registered with the Board and "data-blind". They act as fiduciaries for individuals and let them give, manage and withdraw consent across fiduciaries. Registration opens 13 Nov 2026 — [Khaitan](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/ERGO%20-%20Digital%20Personal%20%20Data%20Protection%20Rules%20-%2015%20November%202025.pdf); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/breaking-govt-notifies-final-dpdp-rules-sets-staggered-rollout-with-key-obligations-for-data-fiduciaries-84201.htm)
- **Penalties.** Up to Rs 250 crore for serious failures — [The Week, 20 Dec 2025](https://www.theweek.in/theweek/business/2025/12/20/indias-dpdp-rules-2025-a-deep-dive-into-the-new-data-privacy-law.html)

### Inferences
- **Roles and contracts.** The developer or brokerage client is the Data Fiduciary for leads and chat logs; the SaaS platform is a Data Processor. The product's DPA and sub-processor list (Meta/WhatsApp, LLM provider, cloud) should reflect this. The fiduciary stays liable, so clients will expect contractual security commitments and 72-hour breach support from the vendor.
- **Consent UX (web forms, chatbot, Meta Instant Forms).**
  - Show a standalone notice listing the data fields (name, phone, email, budget, preferred configuration, chat transcript). Map each to a purpose: "to contact you about Project X", "to score your enquiry", "to send WhatsApp updates".
  - Use separate opt-ins for marketing versus service messages. Include a withdrawal link.
  - Lead scoring and profiling should be named as a purpose in the notice.
- **Retention.** Real estate CRMs are not in the Third Schedule classes, so no fixed 3-year maximum applies. The general rule still holds: erase once the purpose is served or consent is withdrawn, but keep processing logs for at least 1 year. Configure per-client retention policies, for example auto-purging cold leads after N months with 48-hour pre-erasure notice as good practice, while keeping access and processing logs for at least 12 months.
- **Withdrawal propagation.** Withdrawal must cascade to WhatsApp opt-out, telecaller DND flags and Meta custom-audience uploads.
- **Phasing.** The hard legal deadline for most obligations is May 2027. Enterprise developers are already asking for DPDP-ready vendors, so design to the Rules now.
- **Children.** Lead forms should include an "I am 18+" affirmation to avoid triggering parental-consent obligations.

### Gaps
- Full DPDP Act penalty schedule (other than the Rs 250 crore cap): from memory, Rs 200 crore for breach notification and children, Rs 150 crore for SDF duties, Rs 50 crore residual. Not verified in this session.
- Whether any Significant Data Fiduciary notification has been issued, and any cross-border negative list, as of Nov 2026: not found.
- Whether the Data Protection Board is operational and has issued guidance on legitimate use for B2B lead resale or sharing between developer and channel partners: not researched.

## 3. India TRAI telemarketing / DND rules (TCCCPR 2018, 2025 amendment, 140/1600 series)

### Takeaway
The February 2025 TCCCPR amendment hardened India's anti-spam regime for real estate telecallers:
- Promotional voice calls must come from the 140 series. Service and transactional calls move to 1600. Ordinary 10-digit numbers are barred for telemarketing.
- First-time violators lose outgoing services for 15 days; repeat offenders face 1-year disconnection and blacklisting.
- Complaints can be filed up to 7 days after the spam.
- After an opt-out, consent cannot be re-sought for 90 days.

A lead CRM that triggers telecaller calls must enforce DND/consent checks and must not let agents cold-call from personal 10-digit SIMs.

### Cited Findings
- **The amendment (announced 13 Feb 2025):**
  - 140 series for promotional calls; 1600 series for transactional and service calls; 10-digit numbers banned for telemarketing.
  - Complaint window extended from 3 to 7 days. Investigation time cut from 30 to 5 days.
  - Action threshold for unregistered senders cut from 10 complaints in 7 days to 5 complaints in 10 days.
  - Sanctions on senders: first violation means all outgoing telecom resources barred for 15 days; subsequent violations mean disconnection for 1 year and blacklisting across operators.
  - Every promotional message must carry an opt-out. After opting out, consent cannot be re-requested for 90 days. Consent tied to an ongoing transaction expires after 7 days.
  - Access providers face fines of Rs 2 lakh, Rs 5 lakh, then Rs 10 lakh per instance, and may take security deposits from senders.
  - Effective 30 days after Gazette publication; some regulations after 60 days.
  - Source: [FoneArena summary of TRAI amendment](https://www.fonearena.com/blog/446114/trai-tcccpr-2025-tougher-rules-spam-calls-sms.html); [TRAI Press Release No. 11 of 2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf) (official PDF; fetch failed, contents corroborated via search snippet: "140 series will continue to be used for promotional calls, the newly allocated 1600 series is…")
- **Later clarification.** TRAI subsequently clarified the roles of the two series: 140 is promotional for any sector; 1600 is for service and transactional calls (BFSI and government) — [EaseMyPrep summary of PIB release](https://easemyprep.in/news/2902-trai-releases-clarifications-regarding-functioning-of-designated-1600-series-and) (secondary; dated mid-2026)
- **AI-driven telemarketing.** Commentary on TRAI's crackdown on spam calls and AI-driven telemarketing exists — [Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/trai-s-crackdown-on-spam-calls-and-ai-driven-telemarketing) (not fetched; title only)

### Inferences
- **Telecaller workflow.** The CRM's "call lead" action should route through a registered 140-series telemarketer or CPaaS for promotional calls. For calls to a lead who has just enquired (transactional or explicit consent), keep a timestamped consent record with the source (form, chatbot, Meta lead form) to defend against UCC complaints.
- **Consent logging is the core defence.** Store consent artefacts per lead: notice version, timestamp, channel and IP or Meta lead ID. Because transaction-linked consent lapses after 7 days, flag leads older than 7 days with no ongoing transaction or explicit marketing consent as "do not cold call".
- **Opt-out handling.** "Not interested" or "stop calling" captured by a telecaller or chatbot must set a 90-day no-re-solicitation lock.
- **Brokerage risk.** Brokerages using agents' personal mobiles for cold calls risk 15-day bars and 1-year disconnection of the SIMs. This is a real pain point the product can address with click-to-call through registered headers, or by shifting outreach to WhatsApp after opt-in.

### Gaps
- The text of the 2025 Gazette notification itself was not retrieved; trai.gov.in refused the connection.
- DLT (Distributed Ledger) header and template registration rules for SMS, and any 2025-2026 changes to the Digital Consent Acquisition (DCA) facility, were not verified.
- Whether TRAI or DoT rules treat AI voice bots specifically (for example, a disclosure requirement) was not found.

## 4. UAE (Dubai DLD/RERA, Trakheesi, Madmoun, broker display, off-plan/escrow; Abu Dhabi ADREC; Media Council)

### Takeaway
Dubai requires a RERA/Trakheesi advertising permit for every property ad, including social media and property portals, with the permit number and Madmoun QR displayed. Since DLD Circular 2-2022 (20 Jul 2022), off-plan ads must show the developer name, project name, project number, location and **approved escrow account number**. **Direct marketing calls are prohibited unless the person is on the RERA "green list".** Abu Dhabi requires a Madhmoun permit via DARI from 7 Jul 2025; ADGM has had its own permit since Aug 2025. Separately, the UAE Media Council's Advertiser Permit (enforced from about 1 Feb 2026) applies to individuals who promote on social media.

### Cited Findings
- **DLD/RERA Circular (2-2022), 20 Jul 2022, ref. DLD/OUT/2022/0004542**, "Real Estate ads Terms and conditions". It applies to all channels "including real estate platforms or social media":
  1. An official RERA permit must be obtained and its number displayed on the ad.
  2. The ad must include the real estate company's name.
  3. Off-plan ads must include the developer's name, project name, project number, project location and approved escrow account number.
  4. The advertiser is responsible for the accuracy of content, and the ad must not include information not approved in the permit.
  5. Promotional campaigns (free benefits, value-added facilities, discounts, fee exemptions) need a separate promotional campaign permit.
  6. "It is strictly forbidden to market or promote through direct calls unless the person is registered in RERA-approved green list."
  - Violations will be issued for non-compliance.
  - Source: [DLD circular PDF (bilingual)](https://dubailand.gov.ae/media/daylptgk/الشروط-والاحكام-الخاصة-بالإعلانات-العقارية-1.pdf) (text extracted from official PDF); [Lexis.ae summary](https://www.lexis.ae/2022/07/26/dubai-requirements-for-real-estate-advertisements-announced/)
- **Madmoun QR.** Became mandatory from 24 Apr 2023. A QR code is generated per Trakheesi ad permit; scanning shows the advertiser, permit status and property details — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/business/property/dubai-land-department-launches-madmoun-service-for-real-estate-ad-verification-1.95202094); [Zawya / DLD press release](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/government-news/dld-madmoun-re-ads-service-enhances-trust-and-transparency-in-the-real-estate-sector-y3prxb20)
- **Penalties (Dubai).**
  - Reported fines of AED 50,000 per advertising violation, which may escalate to licence cancellation — [Gulf Business](https://gulfbusiness.com/?p=173376); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/government-news/dld-madmoun-re-ads-service-enhances-trust-and-transparency-in-the-real-estate-sector-y3prxb20). These figures come from 2020-2023 reporting; the current schedule is unverified.
  - A developer was fined AED 50,000 for collecting money outside escrow and running a promotional campaign without permits — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/property/dubai-s-real-estate-regulator-slaps-company-with-dh50-000-fine-for-breaching-escrow-rules-1.1017070)
- **Social media.** Dubai property ads, including social posts, need RERA approval — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/property/real-estate-brokers-in-dubai-now-need-to-gain-approval-for-property-ads-1.185121). The DLD now uses AI to detect and correct listings — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-property-ads-land-department-gets-ai-to-correct-listings-1.500104134)
- **Off-plan framework.**
  - Law No. 8 of 2007 requires a dedicated escrow account per off-plan project; buyer money must flow through escrow.
  - Law 13 of 2008 requires project registration with DLD before SPAs or collecting payments.
  - Developers are responsible for their brokers' advertising compliance, and RERA can act against both.
  - Source: [BSA Law](https://www.bsalaw.com/insight/navigating-dubais-off-plan-real-estate-laws-compliance-essentials-for-developers/); [Kayrouz & Associates](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/rera-developer-obligations-off-plan-dubai)
- **Abu Dhabi (ADREC / DMT).**
  - The Madhmoun ad permit through the DARI platform has been mandatory since 7 Jul 2025 for all property types. Only ADREC-licensed brokers and companies may apply; owner approval is needed, and listing data is verified against official records — [Lexis.ae, 28 Aug 2025](https://www.lexis.ae/2025/08/28/auh-launches-new-real-estate-advertisement-permit-service/); [Bayut support](https://support.bayut.com/hc/en-us/articles/27885350655634-What-is-a-Madhmoun-Permit)
  - Law No. 3 of 2015 bars acting as developer or broker without authorisation. A developer was suspended for promoting unlicensed projects — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/real-estate-developer-suspended-in-abu-dhabi)
  - ADGM (Al Maryah and Al Reem Islands) launched its own advertising permit via AccessRP in Aug 2025 — [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/news/abu-dhabi-real-estate-new-transparency-policies-introduced-by-adrec-and-adgm/); [Aletihad](https://en.aletihad.ae/news/business/4599498/permits-mandatory-for-all-real-estate-ads-within-adgm-jurisd)
- **UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit** (Federal Decree-Law 55/2023 on Media Regulation; fines under Cabinet Resolution 42/2025):
  - Launched 30 Jul 2025. Required for anyone publishing promotional content on social media, paid or unpaid.
  - Enforcement reported from 1 Feb 2026. Reported fines range from AED 10,000 up to AED 1 million depending on severity and repetition.
  - Real estate is a "specialised" sector needing prior approval; a Trakheesi permit may be needed in addition. The permit number must be shown on the profile.
  - Source: [The National, 30 Jul 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2025/07/30/uae-launches-new-advertising-regulation-for-influencers); [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/technology-edition/articles/uae-introduces-mandatory-advertiser-permit-for-content-creators/); [Gulf News on fines](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/telephone-internet/what-fines-influencers-and-content-creators-face-under-uae-media-law-1.500418948); [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/news/uae-influencer-licence-deadline-looms)
- **AI imagery (Media Council).** On 25 Sep 2025 the Council said that depicting national symbols or public figures with AI without prior approval violates media content standards, and that AI misuse for misinformation is a media offence — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/blvxe9y-uae-media-council-warns-against-misuse-harmful)

### Inferences
- **Permit gating.** The ad/listing builder must require a valid Trakheesi permit number (Dubai), Madhmoun permit (Abu Dhabi mainland) or ADGM permit before publishing to Meta or the website. It should auto-render the permit number and Madmoun/Madhmoun QR, the company name and, for off-plan, the developer, project number, location and escrow account number. Track permit expiry and auto-pause ads when a permit lapses ("expired permit" is a commonly cited violation).
- **Content lock.** The chatbot and ad copy must not state anything not approved in the permit (Circular 2-2022 clause 4). Payment-plan offers, "DLD fee waived", "free service charges" and similar promotions require a separate promotional campaign permit. The bot must not invent or upsell promotions.
- **Outbound calls in Dubai.** Circular 2-2022 forbids direct marketing calls to anyone not on the RERA green list. Combined with TDRA rules (section 6), this pushes UAE outreach toward inbound and opt-in WhatsApp rather than cold calling. The CRM should default UAE leads to "no cold call" unless consent is recorded.
- **Influencer and agent accounts.** Agents posting listings from personal Instagram accounts may also need a Media Council Advertiser Permit, which adds friction for brokerages running Meta ads through agents' pages.

### Gaps
- No primary source was found for a specific rule requiring display of the broker's BRN (RERA broker card number) or company ORN on ads. Trade practice (portals display BRN and ORN) suggests it, but it is not verified.
- No current (2025-2026) official DLD penalty schedule for advertising violations was found; the AED 50,000 figure is from older reporting.
- Details of the RERA "green list" mechanism (how consumers register, current operation) were not found.
- Whether ADREC requires escrow details in off-plan ads (equivalent to Dubai clause 3) was not verified.

## 5. UAE PDPL, DIFC/ADGM data protection, cross-border transfers

### Takeaway
The federal PDPL (Federal Decree-Law 45/2021) is in force on paper, but its executive regulations still do not appear to have been formally published as of mid/late 2026. The six-month grace period runs from their issuance, so federal enforcement remains uncertain. Vendor claims of a 1 Jan 2027 deadline are unverified. Free zones run separate regimes. DIFC's Regulation 10 on AI and autonomous systems has been fully enforced since January 2026 and requires notice to users when AI processes their personal data. That matters for DIFC-based brokerages or a DIFC-hosted vendor.

### Cited Findings
- **Executive regulations not published:**
  - As of 6 Jan 2025 — [DLA Piper Data Protection](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=AE)
  - A Chambers 2026 guide states they had not been issued; recording-law reports none as of May 2026; BusinessDubai (Aug 2026) says not issued, with conflicting decision numbers circulating — [BusinessDubai](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/uae-pdpl-data-protection); [Recording Law](https://recording-law.vercel.app/world-data-privacy-laws/uae-data-privacy-laws)
  - Contradicted by low-authority vendor pages claiming issuance in 2026 with a 1 Jan 2027 deadline and no decision number — [PromptQuorum](https://www.promptquorum.com/local-llms/uae-pdpl-data-sovereignty-local-ai-2026)
  - The six-month compliance window runs from issuance of the regulations — [Cybersigma](https://cybersigmacs.com/knowledge-center/uae-pdpl/)
- **Scope.** The PDPL excludes free-zone entities with their own data protection law (DIFC DP Law 5/2020, ADGM DPR 2021), though one source reads the scope more broadly. Consent and legitimate interests are the main legal bases among several — [Modulos](https://docs.modulos.ai/frameworks/uae-pdpl); [Cybersigma](https://cybersigmacs.com/knowledge-center/uae-pdpl/)
- **Cross-border (PDPL Art. 22–23).** Transfers to "adequate" jurisdictions, otherwise contractual measures or explicit consent. The UAE Data Office has not published an adequacy list, so in practice contractual clauses are used — [Kayrouz & Associates, 2026](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/cross-border-data-transfers-under-uae-law-in-2026); [trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/united-arab-emirates-allows-cross-border-data-flows-personal-data)
- **DIFC and ADGM transfers.** DIFC uses a regulation-listed adequacy list. ADGM recognises EU-adequate jurisdictions plus DIFC. DIFC-to-mainland transfers need safeguards (SCCs or BCRs) — [Kayrouz & Associates](https://www.kayrouzandassociates.com/insights/cross-border-data-transfers-under-uae-law-in-2026)
- **DIFC Regulation 10 (autonomous and semi-autonomous systems).**
  - Enacted Sept 2023; full enforcement from Jan 2026.
  - Deployers and operators must notify users when they first use an app or website that uses such systems to process personal data, and must explain the processing in non-technical terms.
  - High-risk commercial use requires an Autonomous Systems Officer; certification requirements are reported.
  - Fines under Schedule 2 of the DPL.
  - Source: [Mayer Brown, 19 Jan 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/01/ai-regulation-in-the-difc-personal-data-processed-through-autonomous-and-semi-autonomous-systems); [Clyde & Co, Sept 2023](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2023/09/difc-enacts-landmark-regulation-autonomous-systems); [Baker McKenzie](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/emea/dubai-international-financial-center/topics/artificial-intelligence-profiling-and-automated-decision-making)

### Inferences
- **Design to a GDPR-like baseline for UAE mainland clients:** consent or legitimate interest recorded per lead, a privacy notice, data subject access and deletion, and contractual transfer clauses. This is low-regret whatever the executive-regulation timing.
- **Data residency.** Storing UAE leads on servers in India or the US, or sending chat text to a US LLM API, is a cross-border transfer. With no adequacy list, use contractual safeguards plus disclosure, or offer a UAE-region hosting option for enterprise developers (several large Dubai developers are government-linked and may demand in-country hosting).
- **DIFC.** If the client (or vendor) is a DIFC entity, the AI chatbot must show a Regulation 10 notice at first interaction disclosing AI processing of personal data, and keep an explainability record. Lead scoring may qualify as automated processing.

### Gaps
- PDPL penalty amounts: the federal law defers fines to the executive regulations or Cabinet decisions; none confirmed.
- Whether TDRA's or the UAE Data Office's operational status changed in late 2026: unverified.
- ADGM-specific AI rules: not researched.

## 6. UAE TDRA telemarketing rules (2024)

Cabinet Resolutions 56 and 57 of 2024 (effective mid-August 2024) restrict telemarketing in the UAE to **9am–6pm**, require registered numbers and appropriate channels, and carry fines up to **AED 150,000** per violation. Dubai's real estate circular separately bans direct marketing calls to anyone not on the RERA green list. Cold-calling property leads in the UAE is high-risk.

### Cited Findings
- **The resolutions.** Cabinet Resolutions 56 and 57 of 2024; calls allowed only 9am–6pm; effective mid-August 2024 — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/advertising-marketing-branding/1495836/new-telemarketing-regulations-introduced-in-uae); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/new-telemarketing-rules-in-uae-timings-fines-exemptions-explained)

The bands differ by source: AED 10,000–75,000 for out-of-hours calls; AED 5,000–150,000 overall; AED 25k/50k/75k escalation for unregistered numbers — [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/developments/thought-leadership/new-telemarketing-rules-and-regulations-in-the-uae/); [Mondaq guide to fines](https://www.mondaq.com/telecoms-mobile-cable-communications/1532298/new-telemarketing-rules-and-regulations-in-the-uae-a-comprehensive-guide-to-fines-and-enforcement)

### Inferences
- **Configure the CRM dialer and WhatsApp outbound per country:**
  - UAE: 9am–6pm local time window enforced; calls only from company-registered numbers; record consent.
  - Dubai: no unsolicited calls without green-list status.
- **Inbound-only for the UAE.** Leads generated by Meta ads or the chatbot are inbound (they requested contact). The consent artefact (lead form disclaimer text plus timestamp) is the key evidence that a follow-up call is not cold telemarketing.

### Gaps
- Not verified against the official Cabinet Resolution text:
  - DNCR (Do Not Call Registry) check obligations
  - call-recording requirements
  - the "no re-call after refusal" rule and per-day or per-week call limits
  - prohibition on using personal numbers
- The exact fine schedule per violation type is inconsistent across sources.

## 7. Meta Ads policies (Special Ad Category, targeting, Lead Ads data terms, 2025 changes)

### Takeaway
Meta's Housing Special Ad Category restrictions are **mandatory only for ads reaching the US, Canada and Europe**. For India and UAE audiences, advertisers must still fill the special-ad-category field but may select NONE, so full detailed targeting remains available. However, detailed-targeting **exclusions** were removed for all advertisers in 2025. Lead data from Instant Forms is governed by the advertiser's own privacy policy and Meta's Business Tools terms: no selling audiences, no sensitive data, and consent and notice are the advertiser's responsibility.

### Cited Findings
- **Who must use the Housing category.** Restrictions apply to businesses in or reaching the US, and to those reaching Canada (from 3 Dec 2020) or Europe (from 7 Dec 2021). Businesses outside the US reaching audiences outside the US, Canada and Europe "must still pass the Special Ad Category field, but they can opt out by using NONE". India and the UAE are not listed — [Meta Marketing API docs, Special Ad Category](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/audiences/special-ad-category)
- **Housing restrictions where they apply:**
  - Age fixed at 18–65+; no gender selection.
  - No location exclusion; minimum 15-mile/25km (US/CA) or 15km (EU) radius; no ZIP or neighbourhood targeting.
  - No behaviour or demographic targeting; no lookalikes.
  - Home Listing catalog ads must specify HOUSING.
  - Source: [Meta Marketing API docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/audiences/special-ad-category)
- **Categories as of Oct 2026.** Meta's categories are housing; employment; financial products and services (replaced "credit" in Oct 2024); and social issues, elections or politics — [AdAdvisor](https://adadvisor.ai/blog/meta-special-ad-categories); [Zag Interactive](https://www.zaginteractive.com/insights/articles/january-2025/2025-changes-for-meta-ads)
- **Detailed targeting exclusions removed.** Meta announced on 21 Jan 2025 that new ad sets would lose detailed targeting exclusions; dates for existing ad sets vary by source — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-removes-detailed-targeting-exclusions-for-ad-accounts/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/meta-eliminates-detailed-targeting-exclusions-6802509). Custom-audience exclusions are unaffected — [search summary of 2026 source](https://leadenforce.com/blog/facebook-ads-in-2026-what-targeting-will-look-like)
- **Business Tools terms (covering pixel, CAPI, custom audiences):**
  - The advertiser warrants it has all rights, permissions and legal basis to share data.
  - A clear, prominent privacy notice is required where pixels are used; cookie consent where required by law.
  - Custom audiences must not be sold or transferred.
  - No unhashed contact info where hashing is required; no health, financial or other sensitive data.
  - Contact info is used only for matching and deleted after; event data is kept up to 2 years.
  - Source: [Meta Business Tools Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/terms/businesstools)
- **Lead Ads (Instant Forms).**
  - Requires a live privacy-policy URL. A custom disclaimer with checkboxes is optional, and users must accept it to submit.
  - Lead Ads Terms (last modified Oct 2025, per a secondary source) bar targeting minors and collecting sensitive or prohibited information.
  - Source: [Klaviyo help](https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/14318416590235); [LeadEnforce on Lead Ads Terms](https://leadenforce.com/blog/about-lead-ads-terms-and-security-what-advertisers-must-know); [iubenda](https://www.iubenda.com/en/help/12158-privacy-policy-facebook-lead-ads)

### Inferences
- **India and UAE campaigns** can use interest, behaviour and demographic targeting, and lookalikes (for example HNI or NRI interests, Indian expats in the UAE), provided the campaign does not reach US, Canada or EU audiences. **NRI-targeting campaigns that include the US, Canada, UK or EU geos trigger Housing SAC restrictions.** The product's campaign builder should detect geo mix and force HOUSING plus restricted targeting when those geos are included. This is a practical design rule for Indian developers selling to NRIs.
- **Lead form template.** Every Instant Form the product generates should include:
  - the client's privacy policy URL;
  - a custom disclaimer reproducing the DPDP-style notice (data fields, purposes, WhatsApp/call consent, withdrawal link);
  - separate optional checkboxes for WhatsApp marketing and phone calls. These double as TRAI, TDRA and WhatsApp opt-in evidence.
  - Also store the Meta lead ID and form version with each lead.
- **CAPI and offline-conversion uploads** of lead-quality scores back to Meta are allowed under the Business Tools terms, but must carry no sensitive data. Do not send income, loan status or religion inferred by the scoring model as event parameters.

### Gaps
- Meta's official Business Help Center page on special ad categories (country list) did not render, so it could not be cross-checked against the developer docs.
- No India- or UAE-specific Meta real-estate ad policy changes in 2025-2026 were found.
- The text of the "Lead Ads Terms" (facebook.com/legal) was not retrieved directly.

## 8. WhatsApp Business Platform (pricing, 24h window, templates, opt-in, AI chatbot policy, marketing caps)

### Takeaway
Since 1 Jul 2025, WhatsApp bills **per delivered template message** by category (marketing, utility, authentication) and recipient country. There have been further changes since:
- Utility messages sent inside the customer-service window became chargeable from **1 Oct 2026** (previously free).
- Service (free-form) messages are free only for the first 1,000 per number per month, from Oct 2026.
- India marketing rates rose on 1 Jan 2026; UAE marketing rates rose on 1 Oct 2025 and 1 Oct 2026.
- India billing must migrate to INR by 31 Dec 2026.

From 15 Jan 2026, the WhatsApp Business Solution Terms ban **general-purpose AI assistants** where the AI is the product. **Business-specific assistants (support, lead qualification, property Q&A) remain allowed**, but must offer a clear human escalation path. Meta also caps marketing templates per user (error 131049).

### Cited Findings
- **Pricing (official):**
  - Per-message pricing since 1 Jul 2025, charged per delivered message by category and recipient market.
  - Categories: Marketing, Utility and Authentication (templates, sendable any time); Service (non-template, only within the 24h window).
  - "Meta Business Agent" was introduced 1 Jul 2026, billed per token at USD 2.00 per million tokens.
  - Utility messages in response to users were free from 1 Jul 2025 but **are charged at published rates from 1 Oct 2026**.
  - Service messages: first 1,000 per business phone number per month free, then charged; a payment method is needed after 1 Oct 2026.
  - Source: [Meta WhatsApp pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **24-hour window.** Opens when a user messages or calls and resets with each user message. Outside it, only approved templates may be sent — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing); [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- **Free Entry Point.** A user who starts a chat from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad opens a window of up to 7 days in which marketing, utility, authentication and service messages are free (Meta Business Agent messages still bill) — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **Volume tiers** apply to utility and authentication only, aggregated monthly per business portfolio — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **India:**
  - Marketing rates increased 1 Jan 2026; authentication-international rates increased 1 Apr 2026.
  - Billing moves to INR: accounts must migrate by 31 Dec 2026 or delivery stops from 1 Jan 2027.
  - Rate amounts are in Meta's rate-card CSVs, not on the page.
  - Source: [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
  - A vendor quotes India utility and authentication at about Rs 0.13 per message, but its marketing figure is internally inconsistent (Rs 0.13 vs Rs 0.86) — [MyOperator](https://support.myoperator.com/portal/en/kb/articles/whatsapp-has-shifted-to-per-message-pricing-effective-july-1-2025-based-on-message-categories-and-your-recipient-s-country-in-india-there-are-three-paid-categories)
- **UAE.** Marketing rates increased 1 Oct 2025 and 1 Oct 2026; authentication rates were lowered 1 Feb 2025 — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **AI providers ban:**
  - The WhatsApp Business Solution Terms bar "AI Providers", whose AI or chatbot is the primary functionality, from using the Business API. Applies to new users from 15 Oct 2025 and all users from 15 Jan 2026.
  - ChatGPT, Perplexity, Luzia and Poke exited; Meta AI remains.
  - Businesses using AI within customer-service workflows are not affected.
  - Source: [TechCrunch via Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meta-bans-ai-chatbots-from-whatsapp-business-api-chatgpt-to-shut-down-jan-2026-125102200335_1.html); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/10/20/whatsapp-bans-ai-chatbots-from-using-its-business-api/)
- **What is allowed.** Structured and business-scoped tasks (support triage, appointment reminders, lead qualification and routing) where the AI is "ancillary to a legitimate business service". Banned: open-domain assistants answering arbitrary questions or acting as companions. Grey-area "concierge" bots should stay on-topic and offer quick human handoff — [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)
- **Messaging Policy (last updated 23 Sep 2026):**
  - Contact only people who gave their number and opted in; separate opt-ins per message category recommended.
  - Honour all opt-out and block requests, on or off WhatsApp.
  - Automation is allowed within the 24h window but must offer "prompt, clear, and direct escalation paths" (human agent transfer, phone, email, etc.).
  - Templates only outside the window.
  - Do not use data obtained from WhatsApp about a person beyond supporting the messaging (excluding thread content). Do not forward chat information to other customers.
  - Never request full card, financial account or personal ID numbers.
  - Maintain a published privacy policy.
  - No wrongful discrimination by family or marital status, etc.
  - Volume limits can apply based on blocks and reports. The policy text does not mention AI or LLMs.
  - Source: [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)
- **Opt-in requirements (developer docs, updated 3 Nov 2025).** The opt-in must state that the person agrees to receive messages from the business, and must name the business — [Meta developer docs, Getting opt-in](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in); [mirror](https://support.chatarchitect.com/books/meta-whatsapp/page/get-opt-in-for-whatsapp-developer-documentation/export/markdown)
- **Per-user marketing cap.** Error 131049 (Cloud API) or 1026 (On-Premises) means the per-user marketing-template limit has been hit. The limit is across all businesses and adapts to a user's recent engagement; wait at least 24h before retrying — [360dialog error reference](https://docs.360dialog.com/api/api-error-message-list); [Chakra](https://chakrahq.com/help/chat/faqs/whatsapp-api-error-code-troubleshooting/whatsapp-error-131049). The claim that "20–25% of Indian marketing messages are affected" is vendor-only and unverified — [DEV/duochat](https://dev.to/duochatio/whatsapp-error-131049-explained-1n6e)

### Inferences
- **The property chatbot is allowed** as long as it is scoped to the developer's projects: floor plans, prices, amenities, site-visit booking, loan-partner handoff. It should refuse off-topic general questions (for example "write my essay") to stay clearly outside the "AI provider" category, and always expose a "Talk to a human / call sales" option.
- **Billing assumptions.** Do not price the product on "free utility messages": from Oct 2026, utility within the window is billed and service messages are free only up to 1,000 per number per month. Click-to-WhatsApp ads remain the cheapest entry, with a 7-day free window. Product design should push Meta ads to CTWA where possible and keep the conversation inside the window.
- **Sensitive data.** The bot must never ask for PAN or Aadhaar in full, Emirates ID numbers, or bank or card numbers on WhatsApp (policy bans full ID and financial numbers). Route KYC and booking payments to a secure web link.
- **Marketing frequency.** Lead nurturing via marketing templates will hit 131049 caps. The CRM should cap marketing templates per lead (for example at most 1–2 per week), prioritise utility (site-visit reminders) and user-initiated sessions, and treat 131049 as a back-off signal, not a retry.
- **India billing.** Indian clients' WABA billing must be migrated to INR before 31 Dec 2026.

### Gaps
- Exact INR and AED per-message rates for marketing, utility and authentication in 2026 were not retrieved; they are in Meta's rate-card CSVs.
- The verbatim text of the "AI Providers" clause in the WhatsApp Business Solution Terms was not retrieved.
- Whether any antitrust action (e.g., EU or Italy) has modified the AI-provider ban was not covered.
- Whether Meta publishes a fixed per-user marketing cap for India was not found; only the adaptive limit is documented.

## 9. AI-specific rules (chatbot AI disclosure, AI-generated property imagery, ASCI code)

### Takeaway
Neither India nor the UAE has a general statutory requirement for a business chatbot to disclose that it is AI. The exception is DIFC (Regulation 10 notice, enforced from Jan 2026). India's IT Rules amendment (notified 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026) requires prominent labelling of "synthetically generated information" (realistic AI-generated or altered audio and visual content). It binds intermediaries, but platforms will pass declaration and labelling duties on to advertisers. AI renders of unbuilt projects are policed indirectly through RERA misrepresentation rules (Sec 12; Goa and TN restrictions on "representational" images and vague claims) rather than an explicit "AI render" rule.

### Cited Findings
- **India IT Rules amendment.**
  - Notified 10 Feb 2026 (G.S.R. 120(E)), in force 20 Feb 2026.
  - Defines "synthetically generated information" as computer-created or altered audio-visual content that appears real, and requires it to be prominently labelled. The draft's 10%-surface-area watermark was replaced by a qualitative "prominent visibility" standard.
  - Takedown timelines shortened (3 hours for certain illegal content).
  - Source: [Hogan Lovells](https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/india-introduces-mandatory-labelling-for-ai-and-3hour-takedown-for-illegal-content); [Business Today, 10 Feb 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/govt-tightens-rules-on-ai-generated-content-mandates-labels-and-faster-takedowns-515505-2026-02-10); [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/amp/story/news/law-policy/centre-amends-it-rules-to-regulate-ai-generated-content-takedown-timelines-reduced); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/social-media/1747176/meity-notifies-the-it-amendment-rules-2026)
- **Goa RERA.** The regulator flagged luxury-facility images shown under a "representational" disclaimer as a practice to curb, and banned disclaimers in ads — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/how-it-works/goa-real-estate-authority-bars-disclaimers-in-property-ads-tightens-rules-on-amenities-79092.htm)
- **ASCI.** A voluntary self-regulatory body with a Code for Self-Regulation and Guidelines for Disclaimers. MahaRERA collaborates with ASCI on ad violations — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/esay-mean-misleading-real-estate-ads-rera-asci.html); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/real-estate-regulatory-authority-collaborates-with-asci-to-identify-violations-in-ads-23498.htm)
- **UAE.** No chatbot-disclosure law was found. The Media Council prohibits AI depictions of national symbols or public figures without approval (25 Sep 2025) and treats AI misinformation as a media offence — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/blvxe9y-uae-media-council-warns-against-misuse-harmful)
- **DIFC Regulation 10.** Requires notice at first use when AI systems process personal data; full enforcement Jan 2026 — [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/01/ai-regulation-in-the-difc-personal-data-processed-through-autonomous-and-semi-autonomous-systems)
- **WhatsApp.** The Business Messaging Policy contains no explicit AI-disclosure requirement — [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)

### Inferences
- **Disclose AI anyway** ("I'm the AI assistant for <Developer>; a sales advisor can take over anytime"). This is mandatory in DIFC, low-cost elsewhere, and reduces misrepresentation risk under RERA Sec 12 and the Consumer Protection Act if the bot misstates a fact.
- **AI renders and 3D walkthroughs.** Label photorealistic AI-generated or AI-altered imagery and video (for example "AI-generated visualisation", "3D rendering based on approved plan"). In India this is needed so Meta, YouTube and other intermediaries don't flag or remove it under the Feb 2026 SGI rules. In TN and Goa, the label must not be a vague disclaimer, so the imagery itself must match the approved plan and promised amenities.
- **Chatbot guardrails.** Answer only from verified project facts (RERA registration data, approved brochure, price sheet, Trakheesi-approved content). No guaranteed returns, ROI or rental-yield promises, no possession dates beyond the RERA-declared date, and no promotions or discounts not covered by a permit (Dubai). Log all bot answers for audit.

### Gaps
- No ASCI real-estate-specific guideline text or ASCI rule on AI-generated imagery was found.
- No India rule (TRAI or MeitY) requiring AI voice-bot or chatbot self-disclosure was found.
- No DLD rule on AI-generated listing images was found; only DLD's use of AI to audit listings is confirmed.
- No Indian Consumer Protection Act / CCPA "dark patterns" guidelines (2023) were researched. They are likely relevant to chatbot and consent UX (false urgency, basket sneaking) but are out of scope here.

Most constraints become a small set of product features:
1. Jurisdiction-aware ad and page disclosure blocks.
