# Competitive Landscape: Lead-Management, AI Chatbot, WhatsApp Automation and 3D/Virtual-Tour Project-Website Vendors for Real Estate Developers and Brokerages in India and the UAE (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-01. Every price has a year label. "(listing, 2026)" means a third-party aggregator listing (Capterra, GetApp, SaaSworthy and similar) viewed in 2026. Many vendors publish no prices, so third-party figures often conflict, and the conflicts are recorded below. Treat any performance number from a vendor's own page or case study as a **CLAIM**.

Source quality note: a large share of the pages that compare Indian real estate CRMs are written by competing vendors (Sell.Do, Leadrat, Codingclave, PropSpace, LaunchpadCRM). They are useful for pricing and feature signals but are biased. They are flagged inline.

## Q1. Real estate CRMs: pricing, lead scoring, WhatsApp, channel-partner (CP) portal, site-visit tracking, Meta/portal lead sync, AI

### Takeaway
The Indian developer CRM market splits in two. Real-estate-specific suites (Sell.Do, Leadrat, PropFlo, Buildesk, Makanify) cost roughly ₹1,000–₹3,000 per user per month and up, and come with CP portals, site-visit tracking and portal lead sync. Horizontal CRMs (Zoho, LeadSquared, Salesforce, HubSpot, Freshsales) need add-ons and integrators for WhatsApp, CP and portals. In the UAE, PropSpace publishes AED-per-user pricing that is broker- and portal-centric, with no lead scoring or AI on its pricing pages. Almost every vendor now markets "AI" (scoring, voice agents), but it is usually a usage-based add-on. No CRM bundles a premium 3D project website.

### Inferences
- The price band for Indian real-estate-specific CRMs converges at about ₹1,000–₹3,000 per user per month (Makanify ₹1,000, Leadrat ₹1,500–₹2,500, Sell.Do around ₹3,000 or $35), with 5–10 user minimums. A 20-seat developer sales team therefore pays about ₹30K–₹60K per month before WhatsApp, telephony and AI usage.
- "AI" in incumbents is moving to usage-based add-ons (Sell.Do Jarvis and voice; Leadrat via Calllive). The incumbents can therefore match a startup's AI scoring feature-for-feature. The differentiation has to come from the bundle and from the buyer-behaviour signals that only the 3D site captures.
- UAE broker CRMs (PropSpace) are listing and portal-syndication tools, not developer off-plan lead-scoring tools. The UAE developer segment has a weaker CRM incumbent than India, where Sell.Do is strong.

### Gaps
- No pricing was found for Goyzer, PropertyBase, Farvision, Propertese, Realtyhub, Bitrix24 or HubSpot (current INR/AED), Housing Edge for builders, or Anarock's internal tech stack. Sell.Do's pricing page says it is backed by Aurum PropTech, and Anarock's relationship to it was not confirmed.
- No official Salesforce Real Estate Cloud India price.
- Setup or implementation fees are rarely published (Leadrat says included, PropSpace says none, TeleCRM ₹1,999). Sell.Do's onboarding fee is unknown.

## Q2. WhatsApp and AI chatbot vendors used in real estate: pricing, case studies, claimed results

### Takeaway
Indian WhatsApp BSP platforms are cheap at the platform level: AiSensy about ₹999–₹2,399/month, Interakt about ₹1,166–₹1,499, Wati $59–$119/month, Gallabox about ₹7,400 or $89–$112. Meta per-message charges come on top, often with a markup. Enterprise conversational AI vendors (Haptik, Yellow.ai) hold flagship developer logos such as Lodha and Godrej. Real estate results are almost all vendor-reported claims.

### Inferences
- At the platform level, WhatsApp automation is commoditized (about ₹1K–₹8K/month). A startup cannot charge a premium for "WhatsApp bot" alone. The value lies in project-specific knowledge (inventory, pricing, floor plans, 3D context) and in passing scored leads into a CRM.
- Enterprise vendors such as Haptik serve top-10 developers with custom deployments. Mid-market developers are underserved between DIY BSP tools and enterprise AI.

### Gaps
- Official current pricing for Gupshup, Verloop, Callbell, Kommo, AiSensy and Interakt was not verified on vendor pages. Most INR figures come from third-party blogs, some written by competitors.
- No independently verified real estate result from any chatbot vendor.

## Q3. AI voice-calling agents for real estate lead qualification (India and UAE)

### Takeaway
A crowded, early field of Indian voice-AI startups (Tenori Labs, Calllive.ai acquired by Leadrat, Gnani.ai, UnleashX, Edesy, Kallix, Vomyra, Girikon) sells Hinglish and regional-language qualification and site-visit booking. CRM incumbents (Leadrat, Sell.Do) are absorbing voice AI as an add-on. No reliable public pricing or independent results were found.

### Cited Findings

- Others selling real-estate voice agents in India: UnleashX ([UnleashX](https://unleashx.ai/feeds/service/ai-voice-agent-real-estate-lead-followup/)), Edesy ([Edesy](https://edesy.in/ai-voice-agent/industries/real-estate)), Kallix ([Kallix](https://www.kallix.in/howto/playbooks/real-estate-india/en)), Vomyra ([Vomyra](https://vomyra.com/blogs/ai-voice-agent-for-real-estate-in-india-a-complete-2026-guide)), Girikon ([Girikon](https://www.girikon.ai/girikvoice/blogs/ai-voice-agents-real-estate-india)), and Everyday Digital Solutions ([EDS](https://everydaydigitalsolutions.com/solutions/real-estate)). ## Q4. 3D, virtual-tour and interactive project-website providers: pricing per project

### Takeaway
At the high end, Dubai and India studios build Unreal Engine digital twins and interactive masterplans for top developers (PropVR, owned by Square Yards; VisEngine; Elipse Studio; Bayut Studios; Virtuelle.io). All are quote-only, and their web delivery is often limited by pixel-streaming costs. At the low end, capture-based tour SaaS (Matterport, Kuula, Floorfy) costs $14–$355/month but suits finished units, not off-plan. Some 3D-first platforms (Vinode, VirtualHomes, ShowMyFlat) add a basic CRM, but none combines premium 3D with AI chat and real lead scoring at a published price.

### Cited Findings
- **PropVR** (Dubai/India; acquired by Square Yards, PitchBook date 17 Mar 2021): pricing is quote-only and varies with size, towers and integrations. Products: Spatial World (3D portfolio for developers with 5+ projects), Spatial Lite (web virtual tour, "deployed at 50+ developers across India, UAE, KSA"), and WebLite (browser 3D tours). — [PropVR WebLite](https://propvr.ai/weblite); [Spatial Lite](https://propvr.ai/platform/spatial-lite); [Spatial World](https://propvr.ai/platform/spatial-world); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/234373-33). **CLAIM**: an Unreal Engine case study credits PropVR's DAMAC digital twin with revenue rising from $40M to $400M ("10x"). — [Unreal Engine spotlight (via search)](https://www.unrealengine.com/spotlights/propvrs-digital-twins-help-real-estate-developer-boost-revenue-by-10x)
- **VisEngine** (Dubai): interactive 3D masterplans for Uptown Dubai (DMCC), Sobha One, Azizi Riviera and Deyaar. ## Q5. Real estate digital marketing agencies in India and Dubai: retainers, CPL, performance pricing

### Takeaway
Agencies in India charge about ₹25K–₹5L+ per month, often a hybrid of a lower retainer plus a per-qualified-lead bonus. Dubai agencies charge about AED 3K–50K per month plus ad spend, with pay-per-accepted-lead models of AED 200–500 per lead. All benchmarks come from agency self-promotion.

### Cited Findings
- India retainers: ₹25,000 to ₹5,00,000+ per month. A common hybrid is ₹40,000 base plus ₹500 per qualified lead. — [BrainGuru 2026](https://blog.brainguru.in/2026/05/digital-marketing-agency-pricing-india/)
- India CPL (2025-2026 agency estimates): affordable housing in Delhi NCR about ₹800–₹2,500; luxury about ₹3,000–₹8,000 per qualified lead. Cost per site visit is ₹3,500–₹12,000 (Bangalore), and site-visit conversion drops to 2–4% with broad keywords. — [BrainGuru real estate playbook](https://blog.brainguru.in/2026/05/real-estate-digital-marketing-agency-india/); [Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/guides/digital-marketing-for-real-estate-india-2026)
- **CLAIM**: Realtica reports a ₹194 average CPL and ₹5,581 per confirmed booking (one project, self-reported). — [Clutch: Realtica](https://clutch.co/profile/realtica)
- Dubai retainers: AED 5,000–50,000 per month; a small brokerage doing portal and social work pays AED 5,000–10,000. — [Real Estate Club Dubai](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/property-management/real-estate-marketing-agencies-dubai-2026-branding-leadgen-compared). Another breakdown gives an AED 3,000–7,000 service fee plus AED 5,000–50,000+ ad spend. A hybrid gives an AED 4,000–12,000 retainer plus AED 200–500 per accepted lead. — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/lead-generation-agency-dubai-quote-traps-2026)
- Dubai CPL benchmarks: Meta AED 30–300; Google AED 450–900 (another source gives AED 80–200); portals AED 30–200. An example brokerage's CPL rose to AED 310 after adding qualifying questions. Bought leads convert at 1–3%, against 5–10% for nurtured ones. — [Kolonell](https://kolonell.com/en/blog/lead-generation-agency-dubai-quote-traps-2026); [Digital Gravity](https://www.digitalgravity.ae/blog/?p=9215); [Clozer](https://clozer.pages.dev/blog/real-estate-lead-generation-dubai.html)
- Lead-gen services in India draw complaints: Trustpilot reviewers of RealEstateIndia.com paid ₹15,000 and ₹27,500 for leads they say were poor. — [Trustpilot](https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/www.realestateindia.com)

### Inferences
- Developers already pay agencies ₹50K–₹5L per month in India or AED 5K–50K in Dubai, mostly for leads whose quality they cannot see. A bundle that scores leads and reports cost per qualified lead and cost per site visit, not just CPL, speaks directly to that pain. The agency budget is also an obvious displacement budget, or the bundle can be sold through agencies as partners.

### Gaps
- No audited or independent CPL benchmarks. Agency landing-page offerings were not priced separately.

### Inferences
Where a bundled startup can win:
1. **3D engagement data turned into a lead score.** No vendor found turns in-site behaviour (units viewed, time on a floor plan, price-sheet opens) into a CRM lead score. VirtualHomes claims "predict likelihood of sale" without evidence. This is the core differentiator.
2. **One bill, no integrations.** The documented complaints are add-on creep and integrator or CSV work. A bundle that includes WhatsApp, the website and the CRM answers them directly.
3. **Published, productized pricing.** 3D studios and Sell.Do are quote-only. A transparent package (setup fee plus monthly) for mid-market developers sits between Unreal studios and agency retainers.
4. **Booking-level attribution.** Sell.Do markets this as the buyer's real question. Reporting cost per qualified lead, per site visit and per booking competes with agencies' CPL-only reporting.
5. **India and UAE together.** Leadrat and PropVR are the only cross-market players found. UAE developer CRMs are weak (PropSpace is broker- and listing-centric, with no scoring or AI).
- Threats: Sell.Do or Leadrat adding a 3D site through a partnership; Square Yards combining PropVR with its CRM and brokerage assets; Bayut Studios bundling 3D with portal leads.

### Gaps
- VirtualHomes, ShowMyFlat and Vinode pricing and customer counts are unknown.
- Whether Square Yards integrates PropVR with a CRM or AI chat for third-party developers was not established.
- Housing.com/Housing Edge, NoBroker and Anarock bundles for builders were not researched in depth.
